# Welcome to Pedal

Pedal turns a WordPress site into a program marketing site for a college, university, school or training provider. It adds a searchable program search, a course catalog and a faculty and staff directory, and it keeps them organized with groupings such as colleges, departments and program types.

## Who this guide is for

This guide is written for **site administrators and content editors**. You should be comfortable signing in to the WordPress dashboard, adding a post or a page, and uploading an image. You do not need to write code, and none of the articles ask you to. However, at times, platform capabilities that are only available to developers are mentioned for the sake of awareness. 

If you are a developer who needs hooks, template overrides, or the PHP API, head over to the [Developer handbook](developers/index.md). This guide deliberately stays on the WP Admin dashboard side of the road.

## Requirements

Before we start, make sure your site meets these requirements:

| Requirement | Minimum |
|---|---|
| WordPress | 6.9 |
| PHP | 8.2 |

You do not _need_ any other plugin but if you want breadcrumbs on your program pages, Pedal works with Breadcrumb NavXT, Yoast SEO, All in One SEO and Rank Math (see [Handle Breadcrumbs](getting-started/look-and-feel.md#handle-breadcrumbs) in _Look and feel_).

## Our running example

Throughout the docs, we will use a fictional school, **Riverbend Community College**. Riverbend offers programs such as *Nursing (ADN)*, *Welding Technology* and *Business Administration*. It has two colleges, the *College of Health Sciences* and the *College of Trades & Technology*, and it employs people such as *Dr. Maya Okafor*, Professor of Nursing.

Riverbend does not exist. Any resemblance to a real institution is a coincidence, and every URL we use on the course ends in `.example` so it cannot point anywhere real. 

## Menu names used in this guide

We describe screens with bold labels and `>` signs. For example, **Pedal > Settings** means: click **Pedal** in the left-hand dashboard menu, then click **Settings** in the submenu that opens. Your theme and your other plugins may add menu items of their own, but these names come straight from Pedal.
